How to Play


To play Thereisnogame, follow these steps:
• Click on the "Play" button to start the game.
• Use your mouse to navigate the game world, collecting resources and building structures as you go.
• Avoid obstacles and enemies that spawn in different parts of the map.
• Gather materials and build new structures to unlock new areas and improve your chances of survival.
